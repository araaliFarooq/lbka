Our Roots & Foundation

The Story Behind LABAIKA Travels

“With over half a decade of excellence and dedicated experience, LABAIKA Travels specializes in crafting spiritually enriching Umrah and Hajj experiences, connecting our clients from across the globe with the heart of Islamic culture.”

Founded in 2018 and proudly rooted in Uganda—the Pearl of Africa—LABAIKA Travels was born from an urgent realization: Muslim travelers, families, and international dignitaries sought authentic adventures and sacred pilgrimages without compromising on their religious ethos, prayer windows, and dietary obligations.

Our deep insight into the global Halaal tourism market ensures that we cater precisely to the unique nuances of modern travelers. Whether leading a solemn contingent of worshipers through the historical sites of Makkah and Madinah, or guiding adventurous travelers through the mist of Bwindi's impenetrable rainforest to witness majestic mountain gorillas, our standard remains singular: world-class warmth, total transparency, and unwavering integrity.
