When is full payment required?

Submitting this request requires no payment. Once you approve your custom itinerary, a nominal deposit secures your permits and accommodation, with balance payments staggered before departure.

Do you process visas for pilgrims?

Yes. As an authorized agency, we directly process all required electronic visas, bio-metric registration advice, and Saudi Nusuk portal verifications directly from our Kampala and Jinja desks.

Can safari meals be guaranteed 100% Halal?

Absolutely. We audit every partner lodge in Bwindi, Queen Elizabeth, and Murchison Falls to guarantee strict Halal-certified meats, alcohol-free culinary prep upon request, and comfortable prayer quarters.
