Umrah: A Sacred Journey of Faith and Forgiveness in Islam

The Islamic pilgrimage known as Umrah holds profound spiritual significance for Muslims worldwide. Unlike Hajj, which has fixed lunar calendar dates, Umrah can be undertaken at any time of year—offering boundless spiritual rejuvenation.

All visitors traveling to Uganda must apply for an electronic visa prior to departure via the official Government portal (visas.immigration.go.ug). Visas are no longer issued on physical arrival at Entebbe International Airport.

If your itinerary combines Uganda with Rwanda or Kenya, consider the East Africa Tourist Visa ($100 USD), which grants multiple entry within the three partner nations for up to 90 consecutive days. Our Kampala concierge can assist with itinerary documentation for visa clearance.

Pilgrims from Uganda may travel on dedicated Umrah visas or Saudi 1-year multiple entry eVisa (depending on nationality status and bilateral accords). All Rawdah Sharif visits in Al-Masjid an-Nabawi (Madinah) must be reserved via the official Nusuk app.

Labaika manages full visa issuance, biometric appointment scheduling, Nusuk slot reservations, and health insurance endorsements under the Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah system.

Yes. An international Yellow Fever Vaccination Card is strictly required for entry into Uganda and re-entry if coming from an endemic zone. The vaccine must be administered at least 10 days before travel.

For Hajj & Umrah pilgrims, the Saudi Ministry requires the quadrivalent (ACYW135) Meningococcal vaccine taken within 3 years (and at least 10 days prior). We advise carrying the physical WHO yellow booklet alongside digital scans on your mobile device.
